# Kit 04 — Readout deck outline (45 minutes, 8 slides)

Presented by Beto to the client decision-maker. Build it in Google Slides. Keep each slide to one
idea, and use the client's own numbers and examples on every slide.

| # | Slide | Content | Time |
|---|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | **Your AI Readiness Score** | One big number (0–100) and the band (🟢 AI-Ready · 🟡 Ready with prep · 🟠 Foundations first · 🔴 Not ready). One sentence on what it means | 3 min |
| 2 | **Two pillars** | Processes & knowledge: X/50. Systems & data: Y/50. One-line diagnosis each | 5 min |
| 3 | **What we found** | The top 5 gaps, each with evidence from the audit or tickets (e.g., "6 of 20 sampled articles contradict what agents do") | 8 min |
| 4 | **If you launched a bot tomorrow** | A concrete story: which questions it would get wrong and why, and what that would cost (CSAT, re-contacts, a wrong answer on money or health) | 5 min |
| 5 | **What AI could handle, and what stays human** | Preview from the ticket sample: 3–5 contact reasons AI could resolve (with % of volume) and 2–3 that should stay with people, and why | 7 min |
| 6 | **Recommended path** | Based on the band: Foundations, Blueprint, or both. What each delivers, and the timeline | 5 min |
| 7 | **Investment** | Range from the rate card. For current clients, the 40% Blueprint discount and the 50% credit toward Build | 4 min |
| 8 | **Next step** | One clear ask: "Shall I send the proposal by [day]?" Plus two kickoff date options | 3 min |
| | Q&A | | 5 min |

## Rules

- **Lead with their numbers, not ours.** No EvolveCX credentials slide. They already know us.
- **Be honest about the score.** A low score is a sale for Foundations, not a reason to soften the findings.
- **Close on one ask.** Send the proposal within 48 hours ([agents/05-proposal-drafter.md](../agents/05-proposal-drafter.md)).
- **After the readout:** move the HubSpot deal to *Readout Delivered*, log the next step, and send a
  one-paragraph recap email the same day.
