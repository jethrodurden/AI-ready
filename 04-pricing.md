# 04 — Pricing

**Principles**
1. **Projects are fixed-fee** (Blueprint, Foundations, Build). The client knows the price before we start.
2. **Keeping things alive is a subscription** (KB & Bot Care). Bots decay without it.
3. **Operations are billed per outcome**, the same unit whether AI or a human resolved it, with a monthly minimum.
4. **Each tier credits part of its price toward the next**, so buying the next tier always looks like the obvious move.
5. All prices are in **USD, before VAT**. For Mexican clients, quote in MXN at a fixed exchange rate
   for the term, plus IVA.

> ⚠️ Cost inputs marked *(assumption)* are placeholders. Replace them with EvolveCX actuals (loaded
> agent cost, productivity per channel, AI platform cost) before quoting. The structure holds either way.

## 4. Tier 3: Hybrid Operations, outcome pricing

### Option 1 (default): price per resolution, AI or human
| Outcome | Chat / WhatsApp / Email | Voice | Notes |
|---------|-------------------------|-------|-------|
| **AI Resolution** | $0.80 (≤ 25k/mo) · $0.70 (25–100k) · $0.60 (100k+) | $1.50 · $1.30 · $1.10 | Includes AI platform and usage costs plus Care |
| **Human Resolution, standard** | $1.75 | $3.25 | Includes AI escalations, with context passed along |
| **Human Resolution, complex** (Tier 2 support, investigations, disputes) | $3.50 | $4.75 | Defined per intent in the Blueprint |
| **Back-office unit** (KYC review, document validation, case closure) | $1.50 – $6.00 per unit | — | Priced per process |
| English / bilingual | +20% on human rates | +20% | |
| **Collections** | % of amount recovered (8–18% by bucket), or per resolution + success fee | | Program-specific |

- **Monthly minimum:** 60–70% of projected monthly billings (covers fixed human staffing and Care).
- **Baseline period:** the first 30–60 days are billed on the current seat/hour model while baselines
  are measured.
- **Volume bands are marginal** (like tax brackets). Only the volume above each threshold gets the lower rate.
- Outcome definitions and quality floors: [frameworks/outcome-definitions.md](frameworks/outcome-definitions.md).

> **Why the human rate is above today's average cost per contact:** once AI takes the simple contacts,
> the ones left for humans are longer and harder (typically 20–40% higher AHT). The client's *blended*
> cost per contact still falls. See §5.

### Option 2: "Digital + Human Agent" capacity pricing (procurement-friendly)
For clients whose procurement can only buy "agents":
- **AI Agent:** $1,200/mo, including up to 1,500 AI resolutions (overage $0.80). The client can mix any
  number of AI and human agents.
- **Human Agent:** current per-position or per-productive-hour price (unchanged BPO model).
- Use this as a bridge for 3–6 months, then convert to Option 1.

### Option 3: shared savings (for large, mature accounts)
- Fix a **target blended cost per contact** (for example, 20% below baseline).
- Bill per resolution as in Option 1. If the blended cost beats the target, the savings beyond it are
  split **70% client / 30% EvolveCX** as a quarterly bonus.

### Legacy option: seats or hours
Keep the current per-position or per-productive-hour model for pure-human programs. Every renewal
conversation now includes an offer of a Readiness Check and an outcome-pricing quote.

## 5. Why outcome pricing doesn't cannibalize us

**Illustrative client:** BNPL, Mexico. 40,000 chat/WhatsApp contacts per month, currently 35 FTE
priced at $1,600/FTE/month.

**Inputs:**
- Loaded EvolveCX cost per agent: $1,100/mo *(assumption)*
- AI variable cost per AI resolution: $0.20 *(assumption)*
- Contacts per agent per month: 1,143 today, falling to 850–950 on the harder residual *(assumption)*
- AI Ops/Care team cost: $3,000/mo *(assumption)*
- Rates: AI $0.80, human $1.75 (flat for simplicity)

| Scenario | AI containment | Client pays / mo | Δ for client | Blended cost/contact | EvolveCX cost | EvolveCX gross margin |
|----------|---------------:|-----------------:|-------------:|---------------------:|--------------:|----------------------:|
| **Today (seats)** | 0% | $56,000 | — | $1.40 | $38,500 | **$17,500 (31%)** |
| Hybrid, month 6 | 45% | $52,900 | −6% | $1.32 | $32,100 | **$20,800 (39%)** |
| Hybrid, month 12 | 60% | $47,200 | −16% | $1.18 | $27,400 | **$19,800 (42%)** |
| Hybrid, month 18 | 75% | $41,500 | −26% | $1.04 | $21,900 | **$19,600 (47%)** |
| ⚠️ *Do nothing: client buys AI elsewhere, cuts our seats to 23* | 45% (theirs) | $36,800 to us | — | — | $25,300 | **$11,500 (31%)** |

**What the table shows**
- The client's cost per contact falls 26% over 18 months, which gives them a credible AI story for the board.
- Our gross margin **dollars rise about 12–19%** and **margin % rises from 31% to 47%**, while revenue per account falls.
- The real comparison is the last row. If we don't sell AI, someone else will, and they'll take
  **about $8–9k/month of margin per account** from us.
- That's before one-time revenue. In this example the Blueprint ($9,500), Build ($28,000), and 25
  Process Records ($12,500) add about **$50k** in project revenue in the first 4 months.
- Freed capacity (agents no longer needed on this account) gets redeployed to new Hybrid clients
  instead of being laid off, which matters for retention and morale.

**Math check (month 12):** AI 24,000 × $0.80 = $19,200 · Human 16,000 × $1.75 = $28,000 → $47,200.
Cost: AI 24,000 × $0.20 = $4,800 · Human 16,000 / 900 = 17.8 FTE × $1,100 = $19,600 · Care $3,000 →
$27,400.
